# Disclaimer and Attribution Standard

This repository documents public blockchain activity for defensive security research and education.

## What the evidence can support

The project may state that:

- an address signed or received a public Ethereum transaction;
- a contract emitted a token event;
- value moved between public addresses;
- multiple addresses share observable funding, deployment, or consolidation relationships;
- a transaction interacted with a known public protocol or service contract.

## What the evidence does not automatically support

The project does **not** infer or claim:

- the legal identity of a wallet owner;
- that a protocol, DEX, bridge, relayer, mixer, or centralized exchange participated in the attack merely because its contracts or wallets appear in a transaction path;
- that a centralized-exchange endpoint identifies a particular customer without records held by the exchange;
- that a Tornado Cash withdrawal reveals its corresponding deposit-side address;
- criminal liability, intent, or legal conclusions about a real-world person.

Labels such as “operator”, “treasury”, “bootstrap”, and “poison address” describe observed on-chain roles in this investigation. They are analytical labels, not identity claims.

The researcher's own wallet, the lookalike address targeting it, and the original triggering transaction are intentionally withheld to preserve privacy.
